Link-bot privacy policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026. Link-bot is a personal-use integration operated by Jincheng Gao, not a public multi-user service.

Access and purpose

After explicit OAuth consent, the integration accesses only Google services and data covered by the owner’s grant. Depending on the selected scopes, these may include email messages and metadata, calendar events, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets, contacts and tasks. Data is used to fulfill the owner’s requests and configured personal automations, such as email summaries and deadline reminders.

Storage and processing

Google OAuth credentials are stored in the owner-controlled gogcli credential store, not in this public website or its repository. Content retrieved from Google may appear in private assistant conversations, summaries, local files and backups on systems controlled by the owner. Retention follows the owner’s assistant history, file and backup settings; the integration does not promise automatic deletion after a fixed period.

Sharing

When needed to fulfill a request, relevant Google content may be sent to the AI model providers configured by the owner. Provider processing and retention depend on the owner’s selected provider and account settings. Link-bot does not sell Google user data, share it for advertising, or use it to develop or train generalized AI models. Google user data is not published on this site. Credentials are used for Google authentication and are not intentionally included in model prompts.

Link-bot’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control, revocation and deletion

The owner may disconnect Link-bot at any time through the Google Account connections page, remove local credentials using gogcli, and delete retained private conversations, files and backups. Revoking Google access stops future API access but does not itself erase previously retained copies.

This documentation website

The site contains static documentation hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no application analytics, login forms or tracking scripts. GitHub may process standard hosting request information under its own privacy statement. Do not post private Google data or credentials in public repository issues.

Contact and updates

For non-sensitive questions, contact the operator through the linked repository. This policy will be updated when the integration’s data practices change.
